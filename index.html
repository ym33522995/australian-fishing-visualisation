People, wellbeing and community

  1. 01 1 in 5

    Adults fished in 2018

    Fishing connects many Australians with recreation and community. In 2018, around one-fifth of Australian adults fished. Participation varied across states and urban-regional communities.

  2. 02 Wellbeing

    An association, not proof

    Frequent fishers reported less low wellbeing in 2019-20. This association does not prove fishing improves wellbeing.

    Spending supports communities

    Beyond individuals, recreational fishing spending supported jobs across Australia.

The section at a glance

From fish locations to trip planning

  1. 01 4 species

    Recorded across Australian waters

    Knowing what gets caught sparks curiosity about fishing locations. Species records provide a starting point for exploring Australia. They reveal recorded locations across coastal and inland waters.

  2. 02 49%

    NSW fisher-days in estuaries

    Understanding fishing places also means considering their aquatic environments. In NSW, estuaries represented 49% of estimated fisher-days. This highlights their importance within that state's fishing activity.

  3. 03 Facilities

    A Moreton Bay example

    Beyond environments, practical facilities help shape a fishing trip. Moreton Bay provides a regional example of supporting infrastructure. Its ramps, jetties, and cleaning stations add practical context.

  4. 04 Depth

    The landscape beneath the water

    Looking offshore, depth contours reveal shelves and deeper waters. These landscapes add another dimension to understanding fishing places. Each map contributes a different perspective on trip planning.
